## Methodology
- Cleaned and merged trading and sentiment datasets
- Created key features like PnL, win rate, trade frequency
- Segmented traders based on behavior and performance
- Analyzed impact of sentiment on trading outcomes

## Key Insights
1. Greed periods show higher profits but higher risk
2. Fear periods increase volatility and losses
3. Frequent traders outperform occasional traders
4. Sentiment strongly influences trading behavior

## Strategy Recommendations
1. Reduce exposure during Fear markets
2. Trade actively but cautiously during Greed
3. Use adaptive strategies based on trader type

## Conclusion
Market sentiment significantly impacts trader behavior and performance. Adapting strategies based on sentiment can improve profitability and reduce risk.
